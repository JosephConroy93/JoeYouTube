# Format: life-story

One documented person's life, told to them in the second person ("you
are him"), in named chapters. Loaded by `script-writer` when `video.md`
says `format: life-story`. The sourcing contract, gates and self-check live
in the agent; this file holds only what is specific to the shape.

## Protagonist

- One real, documented person. The composite-archetype relaxation does not
  apply: everything attributed to "you" must be true of *them*.
- Late stories and legends (a single anecdotal source, a writer decades
  later) are told as the story people will tell, inside the scene, never as
  fact; the research file's tags decide which is which.
- Inner life only as framing of documented acts ("you keep the letter");
  never an invented thought presented as known.
- The name lands in the first minute; the hook is the fall, not the reveal.

## Register

Second person, present tense, dry. Wry and understated, never portentous.
A companion, not a documentary voice. Hedges are dramatised in-scene.

## Opening

- Cold open inside a moment, not at birth.
- The hook question (the open loop the ending pays off) lands in the
  **first 8 seconds** of narration.
- One hard, specific, sourced fact early, delivered casually.
- No CTA before the first reveal; CTA policy comes from `series.md`.

## Chapters

- 8–10 chapters, ~300–400 words each, heading `## Chapter N. <Name>.`
- The name is 2–4 words that open a loop the chapter pays off ("The
  Cardinal's Dog"), never a summary; it is the chapter card's text.
- The heading is shown on the card, not spoken (`chapter.spoken: no`). The
  narration opens however the story needs, but places the viewer in time
  (a year or an age) within its first two sentences.
- Per chapter: what changed, what it cost, one concrete specific only
  someone living it would know, and a hand-off line that opens the next.
- The arc rises then falls; carry a personal thread (family, a friendship,
  a debt) underneath the public one.
- A companion object, when the research gives one, planted before 0:50 and
  returned in the last chapter.

## Ending

The fall, then one reflective beat on what outlived them (a person, a
thing, a habit of the world). Reversal, then the cyclical image, no spoken
moral. Never an outcome the research leaves unknown.

## Runtime

`video.md`'s `runtime_target`. Word count = target minutes ×
`wpm_measured`. Length comes from beats, never padding.

## Score dimensions (Mode 3, 100 points)

| Dimension | Points | Floor |
|---|---|---|
| Opening, first 30 s: hook question in 8 s (8) · hard sourced fact delivered casually (8) · multiple small open loops (7) · voice established immediately (7) | 30 | 18 |
| Ending: reversal then cyclical image (8) · lands what outlived them (7) · does not overstate the research (5) | 20 | 12 |
| Body: escalation holds (7) · chapter names open loops the chapters pay off (6) · personal thread carried (6) · pacing varies (6) | 25 | — |
| Voice: second-person present sustained (5) · dry not portentous (5) · legends told as stories in-scene, never as fact or as meta-commentary (5) | 15 | — |
| Research utilisation: obscure documented specifics over the well-known ones (5) · corrections to common retellings used (5) | 10 | — |

A script below either floor fails regardless of total.
